Same cream collectible character across stages. Readable differences:

- Hatchling: large spotted egg shell, paws together
- Buddy: shell reduced to a small back cap
- Guardian: chest leaf mark, arms held out, more body volume

## Limitations

- Original ChatGPT exports used a black studio field, not a native transparent
  export. Near-black pixels were converted to alpha locally; edge fringing may
  remain.
- Stage scale is similar. Distinction is mainly shell coverage, pose, and the
  Guardian chest mark.
- No third-party license was purchased. Treat these as team-generated hackathon
  assets until a release license is chosen.
- Do not reuse coin marks, tickers, or unverified token branding.
